Our Story

florals designed to feel elevated, modern, and genuinely lived in

Blooms by Bails was created for the kind of gatherings people actually want to be at — thoughtful celebrations, styled details, interactive moments, and florals that feel intentional without feeling overdone.

What We Create

designed for modern hosting

From shower florals and bloom bars to styled tables and custom event moments, the focus is always the same: creating florals that photograph beautifully while still feeling approachable and personal.

We’re drawn to layered color palettes, soft movement, seasonal textures, and setups that feel polished without looking too perfect.

The goal isn’t to create something overly traditional or overly trendy — it’s to create something people remember being around.

the approach

Everything is built around creating floral moments that feel current, intentional, and easy to gather around.

Founder

built from a love of hosting, styling, and florals

Blooms by Bails started from the idea that florals should feel integrated into the event itself — not like something added at the very end.

What began as designing arrangements for personal gatherings quickly evolved into a larger creative outlet centered around flowers, hosting, and curated experiences.

Today, the brand focuses on creating floral moments that feel elevated but still warm, inviting, and fun to be around.

— Bailey
